Our story

Lagoão began as a family farm and opened its gates to visitors who wanted to slow down. Today it combines comfortable lodging with ecotourism, in respect of the land that welcomes us.

Every space uses local wood, stone and plants. The buildings sit within the landscape, like the open window in our logo: the lagoon and the bird are visible from almost anywhere.

  1. Origins
    The farm and the lagoon that gives the place its name.
  2. First guests
    Families and friends begin visiting the property.
  3. Farm hotel
    Chalets, a restaurant and ecotourism activities.
  4. Today
    Responsible tourism, with the products and hospitality of the house.
